Solstice Community Energy builds community-owned solar farms and battery storage across Australia. We are hiring IT and web engineering talent to help local towns manage clean energy.

About Our Mission

Founded in Victoria, Solstice Community Energy partners with regional councils to deliver affordable renewable power. Our digital team builds simple web portals so residents can track daily solar output and community savings.
