Frequently asked questions

Have questions about working with our digital engineering team? Here are answers to common queries.

Are open roles suitable for graduate and early-career developers?

Yes! We actively welcome early-career applicants. Junior engineers receive structured 1-on-1 mentorship, pair programming sessions, and dedicated weekly learning budgets to master semantic web engineering.

How does the digital team ensure accessibility (WCAG AA)?

Accessibility is baked into our development lifecycle from day one. We test with keyboard navigation, screen readers (NVDA/VoiceOver), enforce color contrast minimums, and validate semantic HTML structures before shipping.

Where is the team based and what are the remote working options?

Our main collaboration hub is in Hawthorn, Melbourne (VIC), but all digital positions support hybrid or 100% remote arrangements anywhere within Australian time zones (AEST/AEDT).

What does the hiring and interview process look like?

Our interview process consists of three respectful stages: an initial informal chat about your goals, a practical code review walkthrough (no surprise algorithmic whiteboarding), and a relaxed conversation with the team.
