Frequently Asked Questions

Can students and recent graduates apply?

Yes. Each role on the Jobs page lists its essential and preferable requirements. If you meet the essentials, we encourage you to apply.

Where will I work?

Our teams work in Melbourne and Bendigo with hybrid options, so you can split your week between the office and home.

Can I apply for more than one role?

You can. Submit one application form for each job reference number.
