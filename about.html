Who We Are

We are the digital team at Solstice Community Energy, a clean energy company founded in Victoria. Our company builds solar farms and battery storage to provide affordable power to regional communities across Victoria and New South Wales. We are proud to be 67.67% community-owned and currently power over 36,000 homes.

What Our Digital Team Does

As a team of web developers and IT professionals, we build simple, user-friendly web portals. These web applications allow residents to easily track their daily solar generation and manage their clean power systems.

Life on Our Team

We are an active team of IT students and graduates who work from our Melbourne office in Hawthorn or remotely across Australia. Our junior developers receive weekly code reviews and strong mentorship from senior engineers to help them grow while making a real impact on the community.
