Full-Stack Energy Data Developer

SCE01
  • Reference: SCE01
  • Location: Melbourne, Bendigo, Vic(Hybrid)
  • Salary: A$84,000 - 106,400 + 10.5% super annually
  • Reports to: Lead Software Architect

Role Overview

Design and maintain web applications and real-time telemetry dashboards that empower local households and energy co-ops to monitor solar generation, battery state-of-charge, and wholesale tariff optimisation.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Build responsive,WCAG-accessible customer portals for community microgrid members.
  2. Ingest smart-meter and inverter MQTT telemetry streams into cloud time-series databases.
  3. Collaborate with grid engineers to visualise community battery dispatch schedules.
  4. Maintain automated CI/CD pipelines, API documentation, and unit test coverage.

Essential & Preferable Requirements

  • Essential: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent industry experience.
  • Essential: 2+ years of experience with semantic HTML5, modern CSS, TypeScript/JavaScript, and Python or Node.js.
  • Essential: Demonstrated experience building accessible web interfaces (WCAG 2.1 AA).
  • Preferable: Familiarity with Australian National Electricity Market (NEM) data or AEMO APIs.
  • Preferable: Experience with time-series databases (InfluxDB, TimescaleDB) or Docker.

Grid Integration & SCADA Systems Analyst

SCE02
  • Reference: SCE02
  • Location: Melbourne, Vic (Hybrid)
  • Salary: A$92,000 - 115,000 + 10.5% super annually
  • Reports to: Head of Grid & Digital Operations

Role Overview

Lead the digital integration, SCADA telemetry configuration, and cybersecurity compliance of 5MWh–20MWh community battery energy storage systems (BESS) across regional distribution networks.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Configure and test DNP3, Modbus, and IEC 61850 communications between site controllers and control room SCADA.
  2. Monitor real-time inverter and battery management system (BMS) diagnostics to maximise system uptime.
  3. Automate regulatory reporting and grid performance validation using Python scripts.
  4. Coordinate secure remote-access protocols and network segmentation with the cloud infrastructure team.

Essential & Preferable Requirements

  • Essential: Degree in Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, or Mechatronics.
  • Essential: 3+ years of experience with industrial telemetry protocols, SCADA systems, or IoT edge gateways.
  • Essential: Strong proficiency in Linux networking, firewalls, and Python data scripting.
  • Preferable: Prior experience commissioning solar farms or utility/community battery storage (BESS).
  • Preferable: Working knowledge of the Australian Energy Sector Cyber Security Framework (AESCSF).
